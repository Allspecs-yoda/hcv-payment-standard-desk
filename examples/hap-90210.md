# Worked example — 982.505 HAP on ZIP 90210

Planning only. Not a HAP determination.

24 CFR 982.505(b): HAP = lower of (payment standard − TTP) or (gross rent − TTP).
24 CFR 982.4: gross rent = rent to owner + utility allowance.
24 CFR 982.505(c)(1): family payment standard = lower of voucher-size PS and unit-size PS.

```bash
python3 desk/quote.py --hap 90210 --br 2 --vbr 2 --pct 110 --rent 4000 --ua 200 --ttp 1200
python3 desk/quote.py --hap 90210 --br 3 --vbr 2 --pct 110 --rent 4000 --ua 200 --ttp 1200
python3 desk/quote.py --hap 90210 --br 2 --pct 110 --rent 4000 --ua 200 --mai 4000 --hold-old 5200 --years-in-unit 1
```

FY2026 revised ZIP 90210 2BR SAFMR **$4,350**; 110% = **$4,785**. 3BR SAFMR **$5,520**; 110% = **$6,072**.

## Same-size voucher (2BR family in 2BR unit)

- Rent to owner $4,000 + UA $200 → gross rent **$4,200**
- Family PS = min($4,785, $4,785) = **$4,785**
- TTP **$1,200** (or 30% of $4,000 monthly adjusted income under 5.628(a)(1) only)
- PS − TTP = $3,585; gross − TTP = $3,000 → HAP **$3,000** (gross rent caps)
- Family share **$1,200**; family rent to owner **$1,000**; no utility reimbursement

## 2BR voucher in a 3BR unit

- Family PS = min(2BR $4,785, 3BR $6,072) = **$4,785** — the voucher size, not the extra bedroom, sets the cap
- Same rents → HAP still **$3,000**

## In-place decrease hold (982.505(c)(3))

If the PHA later drops the ZIP schedule from an old family PS of **$5,200** but the family has been in the unit **1 year**, the first reduction may not apply yet (not earlier than two years after the decrease, plus 12 months' notice). `--hold-old 5200 --years-in-unit 1` keeps quoting $5,200 for that in-place family.
